0) Where this verse sits (context + flow)

Disciple’s belief (verse 60):
  • dehyogthi upje = soul arises due to association with body
  • dehviyoge nash = when separated from body, soul gets destroyed Meaning: soul is temporary, “alive as long as the body is alive.”
Verses 62–64 were a build-up:
  • 62: body can’t know anything (no knowingness)
  • 63: soul can’t know its own “birth/death” either (illogical)
  • 64: no combination of matter is observed to create soul → soul is uncreated

✅ Now Verse 65 = direct answer, not “warming up” anymore.

1) Verse 65 (the line the Guru gives)

Main verse line:

“Jadthi chetan upje, chetanthi jad thay;
Evo anubhav koine, kyare kadi na thay.”
Meaning (very clean):
  • Consciousness cannot arise from matter
  • Matter cannot arise from consciousness
  • No one has ever experienced this — anyone, anywhere, anytime

This verse is the “impossible experience” proof.

2) Key definitions (locked-in)

  • Jad = inanimate / matter (pudgal)
  • Chetan = conscious / soul / knowing entity
  • Anubhav = direct experience (not imagination)
  • “Kyare kadi na thay” = never happens, never occurred

✅ He’s making it universal: not just “so far,” but no such experience is possible (unless you want to claim future fantasy).

3) The Guru’s core point: body looks alive, but is still jad

A huge clarification he keeps repeating:

The body does NOT “become living”

  • The body is jad from start
  • It only appears living due to association with soul (bhasyo)

So:

  • When soul leaves → body doesn’t “die into jad” It was already jad; the “life-look” disappears.

✅ This kills the common confusion:

“It was alive, then it became dead, so consciousness turned into matter.” Nope — only association changed.

4) Why the disciple’s theory is imaginary (no evidence)

Guru basically says: You are claiming:

✅ “Consciousness came from matter” ✅ “Consciousness gets destroyed into matter”

But:

  • Nobody has seen it
  • Nobody has experienced it
  • It doesn’t even fit logic

So it’s not a proved truth, it’s assumption + imagination.

5) The “two substances” principle (First Fundamental Truth invoked)

He reminds: this was already taught earlier:

Soul and matter are entirely different substances

  • Each has its own fixed qualities
  • They never merge
  • Trane kaal dvaybhaav = in past/present/future they remain two (dual nature)

So:

  • Matter cannot transform into consciousness
  • Consciousness cannot break down into matter

They can associate, but not convert.

6) Logic used: “Cause must contain effect (even latently)”

This is the big deductive logic rule he uses:

Whatever appears in the effect must exist in the cause

(at least latent / hidden)

So:

  • If matter has no knowingness
  • You cannot produce knowingness by any “processing”
  • Zero knowingness → cannot generate knowingness

✅ No knowingness in cause → no knowingness in effect

7) The famous example: Grapes → wine / sesame → oil

The opponent argument was:

“Grapes don’t have intoxication, but wine does → new property can be created.”

Guru’s refutation:

  • The intoxicating element was latent in grapes It’s manifested by fermentation.

Proof he gives:

  • If you do the same process on something else (banana / lentils / sand), you won’t get wine/oil.
  • So the “new” property wasn’t created from nothing — it was present but unmanifested.

➡ Therefore, consciousness can’t “appear” from matter because matter has no consciousness latent in it.

8) Matter “creation” is also just transformation

He adds a bonus science-aligned point:

Science says matter cannot be created/destroyed → only changes form.

So “production” in the market is actually:

  • changing combinations → new form
  • but not “new substance”

He uses this to reinforce the bigger principle: ✅ Substances do not increase or decrease in the universe. Not matter, not soul.

Population increasing doesn’t mean “souls were created” — it means souls took birth into different bodies due to conditions.

9) The sperm/ova discussion (important but he postpones full detail)

He brings up the temptation:

“Does consciousness arise from consciousness? (parents create child)”

He says: not exactly.

  • Parents create conditions/body-formation
  • The incoming soul comes from previous existence and enters embryo
  • So the soul isn’t manufactured; it arrives and dwells

He uses the “contractor” analogy:

  • Workers build a house → they leave
  • You enter the house
  • They didn’t “create you”

✅ This maintains Verse 65: no conversion/production across substances.

10) The one-line takeaway (exam / revision perfect)

Verse 65 proves: There is no experience of consciousness being created from matter or matter being created from consciousness, because the two are eternally distinct substances, and an effect cannot manifest a property (knowingness) that is not present (even latently) in its cause.
