0) Where we are in the structure

  • Second Fundamental Truth: Soul is eternal (nitya).
  • In 62–63 (1A) Guru showed: No one can know/witness the soul’s “creation and destruction.”
    • Body can’t know (no knowingness).
    • Soul can’t know its own birth/death (needs to be prior + survive).
    • No proven third agency.

Now begins 1B (64–66): proving positively that

  • 64–65: soul is uncreated (not produced by combinations)
  • 66: soul is indestructible And he states a core logical law: “What is not created will not be destroyed.”

1) Opening recap: Soul vs Body (superiority point)

From 62–63:

  • Soul knows birth/death of the body.
  • Body does not know soul’s birth/death (no knowingness).
  • Therefore:
    • Soul is distinct from body
    • Soul is vastly superior due to knowingness
    • Soul knows: itself + body + world

Key takeaway: Knowingness is the defining divider: consciousness > matter.

2) “Argument” is redefined (super important tone)

Speaker clarifies: disciple’s “argument” is not ego-fighting.

  • Not: “prove I’m right / prove you wrong”
  • It is: a truth-seeker asking “Where did my thinking go wrong? Observation? Reasoning?”
  • Sadguru’s goal is not blind belief:
    • Not “just trust me, done.”
    • He wants conviction through understanding.
Definition of Sadguru’s teaching style here:
  • Doesn’t hand you “yes/no”
  • Trains your thinking, not your dependence (“stick in your hand”)
  • Easy/available to a worthy seeker (mumukshu), “busy” to ego-arguer

3) Link statement (the bridge into Verse 64)

Because 62–63 proved:

  • The claim “soul is created/destroyed by association with body” is not backed by:
    • experience (body can’t know)
    • logic (soul can’t know its own birth/death)

Now, in 64–66, Guru will show:

  • Uncreated (64–65)
  • Indestructible (66)

And the “universal law” used:

  • Born → dies
  • Not born → does not die
  • Not created → cannot be destroyed

4) Verse 64 — the line + meaning

Verse line emphasized:

“Je sanyogo dekhiye, te te anubhav drashya; upje nahi sanyogathi, atma nitya pratyaksh.”

Word meanings (as used in the talk)

  • Sanyog: combinations / unions / contacts / associations
  • Dekhiye: seen/observed (in the world)
  • Anubhav: experienced (implies known)
  • Drashya: object that is observed/perceived (by a knower)

Core meaning (what the verse is doing)

1. Whatever combinations we observe in the world (matter mixing with matter),

2. the soul can observe/experience those combinations,

3. yet the soul is not seen arising from any combination,

4. therefore: “atma nitya pratyaksh” = soul is evidently eternal.

Short meaning he gives: All observed unions of matter are knowable to the soul, but none are competent to create soul, so soul is uncreated, hence evidently eternal.

5) The method the Guru is shifting to (Verse 64 method)

After eliminating “who knows soul’s birth?” (62–63), Guru now says:

New homework:

“Look at all visible combinations (sanyog) you can observe/experience. Show me one combination that creates consciousness.”

  • Not limited to “chemistry lab” only
  • Any synthesis / any invention / any science—still won’t produce a conscious knower

This is still deductive logic + experiential observation:

  • You see combinations form bodies, objects, tools
  • You do not see a combination producing knowingness

6) What “uncreated” means here (very precise)

He extends the claim beyond just matter:

  • Soul is not created by:
    • matter (atoms, molecules, body)
    • mind (mentioned as extra clarity)
    • another soul (example: sperm/ova theory)
    • parents (“we produced a baby” = body formation; soul was already there)

Core position stated: Self is a natural substance: self-existent, self-sustaining, self-contained — not a manufactured product of associations.

7) The “combination things disintegrate” illustration (glue / Fevicol)

He gives the intuition:

  • Anything produced by joining parts has:
    • a reason/time for separation
    • decay, disintegration

But:

  • What is not a product of combinations has no inherent reason to disintegrate like that.

So Verse 64 is laying the foundation:

  • Uncreated → no “disintegration fear”
  • (Verse 66 will formalize indestructibility)

8) Cloning section (why it doesn’t refute the verse)

He addresses modern pushback:

  • Cloning is a different technique of procreation (body-production pathway)
  • It does not prove “soul created”
  • If souls could be manufactured, they would be “sold” like products (his satire)

So cloning changes the body-making process, not the “creation of consciousness.”

9) Key conclusions you should carry forward

Verse 64 delivers:

  • The soul is a knower that can observe the world of combinations.
  • No observed combination yields a knower (conscious soul).
  • Therefore soul is not born from associations.
  • Hence: atma nitya pratyaksh (evidently eternal).

And it sets up:

  • Verse 65: will take the argument deeper (he hints: matter↔consciousness creation claims, etc.)
  • Verse 66: “uncreated → indestructible” conclusion becomes explicit.
