0) Where we are in the flow (62 → 67)

The disciple doubted the Second Fundamental Truth:

“Soul is eternal.”

Guru starts resolving it from Verse 62.

✅ Up till Verse 66, Guru used 2 methods:

Method 1 — Questioning the logic (Verses 62–63)

  • Who is the knower of “soul being created/destroyed”?
  • Body can’t know (no knowingness)
  • Soul can’t know its own creation/destruction (it would need to exist before birth and after death)
  • No third agency is available logically

➡ So the doubt’s logic collapses.

Method 2 — Questioning evidence/experience (Verses 64–66)

  • From what association does soul arise? No one can show it (64)
  • Consciousness from matter / matter from consciousness never experienced (65)
  • Uncreated ⇒ indestructible ⇒ eternal (66)

✅ First doubt almost resolved.

1) What changes in Verse 67?

Now Guru strengthens faith through INFERENCE (not experience).

✅ This is the pivot:

  • Earlier: “Show me evidence/experience”
  • Now: “Look at what we observe, and infer the unseen cause.”

2) Verse 67 (main line + meaning)

Key phrase:

“Krodhādi tartamyatā, sarpādiknī māy;
Pūrvajanma sanskār te, jīv nityatā tyāy.”

Word meanings

  • Krodhādi = anger etc. (greed, fear, generosity, etc.)
  • Tartamyatā = variation in degree (more/less: good-better-best)
  • Sarpādik = snakes etc. (scorpions, dogs…)
  • Pūrvajanma sanskār = impressions from past births
  • Jīv nityatā tyāy = therefore the soul’s eternity is proved

✅ Short meaning (one line): Living beings show strong and different tendencies from birth; those tendencies come from past-life impressions, proving the soul existed before this body → therefore the soul is eternal.

3) What is “tartamyatā” (tar–tam)

He explains it with degrees:

  • Good → better → best
  • Gujarati:
    • tar = comparative (“better”)
    • tam = superlative (“best”) ➡ So tartamyatā = variation in intensity (tar–tam = more/less)

✅ Example:

  • Some people get angry instantly
  • Some stay calm even when insulted
  • Same emotion, different “degree”

4) Guru’s key observation in Verse 67

✅ Fact we can SEE:

Some tendencies appear from birth, without training:

  • anger
  • fear
  • greed
  • generosity
  • instincts in animals (food, fear, sex, possession)

Example:

  • Scorpions sting even if you didn’t harm them
  • Dogs know how to bark, reproduce, protect, etc. without being taught

➡ So the Guru says: “Not learnt in this birth, not caused by any incident in this birth… yet present strongly.”

5) The inference logic (smoke → fire model)

He literally teaches the logic structure:

🔥 Inference = knowing the unknown by the known

  • You see smoke
  • You infer fire Because smoke cannot happen without fire.

✅ Here:

  • Smoke = tendencies present from birth
  • Fire = past-life impressions (sanskārs)
  • Conclusion = soul existed before birth → soul is eternal

6) Why this proves eternity (not just past-life)

This is VERY important:

If you accept: ✅ past birth exists → soul existed before this body Then logically: ✅ soul also continues after this body → so it’s not “created with body and destroyed with body”

So verse 67 isn’t “proving rebirth as the main goal” It uses rebirth as a tool to prove the main goal: nityatā (eternity).

7) Science vs spirituality (the debate angle)

He gives the modern comparison:

Science explanation:

Traits are genetically inherited → trace to parents / grandparents

Spiritual explanation:

Traits come from past-life karmas / samskārs → deeper root than genes

✅ His punch question: If genes + same upbringing explain everything…

Then why are siblings / even twins so different? Same mother, same environment, same time, same training — still huge differences.

➡ That “diversity” implies causes beyond just this birth.

8) The “3 conditions” argument (tight logic)

He repeats it in a super clear structure:

We see strong tendencies even though:

1. Not practised / not taught in this birth

2. No incident occurred in this birth

3. Still the tendency is intense from birth

✅ Therefore: It must have been practised in another birth Same soul → different body Impressions carried forward

9) Soul carries “packed tendencies” forward (nice metaphor)

He describes it like luggage:

  • soul picks up tendencies
  • packs them
  • carries them forward
  • unpacks in new body
  • reacts again

Body changes (snake vs human) but the soul’s journey continues unchanged.

10) Practical teaching hidden inside (what this is meant to do)

He doesn’t make it fatalistic — he says:

✅ You can change tendencies through:

  • satsang
  • self-effort (swapurushārth)
  • forgiveness (closing karmic “interest”)

But if you do nothing, tendencies continue automatically.

So this verse also builds:

healthy fear of transmigration

(because one wrong reaction → lower births → huge delay)

11) Clean flashcard summary (perfect revision)

Verse 67 = Eternity by inference

  • Observation: Different degrees of instincts/tendencies appear from birth
  • Not taught, not incident-based
  • Inference: They come from past-life impressions
  • Conclusion: Soul existed before body → will exist after → nitya (eternal)

  • 62–63: “Who witnessed creation/destruction?” (logic collapses)
  • 64–66: “Show creation; show evidence; uncreated = indestructible”
  • 67: “Tendencies from birth → infer past life → soul is eternal”
