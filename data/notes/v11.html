Extensive Study Notes (Pratyaksh Sadguru > Paroksh Jina — by Upkār)

Built from your Video 11 transcript. Designed to read smoothly as a single handout and to be printable as a PDF.

1) Big picture and the precise claim of Verse 11

  • Transition: Verse 10 identified the five inner marks of a Sadguru.
  • Verse 11’s thesis: Among one’s spiritual supports, the obligation (upkār) of a living (pratyaksh) Sadguru is greater (sam nahi = not merely equal) than that of a non-present (paroksh) Jina (Omniscient Lord / Tirthankar).
  • Scope: This does not compare inner states (a Sadguru at 4th/7th etc. vs. a Tirthankar at the 13th stage) but the benefit conferred to you here and now.
  • Critical effect: Without this conviction, true Self-contemplation (ātma-vichār) does not arise. Until then we may talk about the Self but the fire of love for the Self hasn’t been lit.

Takeaway in one line: Conviction in the living Sadguru’s greater upkār is the ignition key for ātma-vichār.

2) Key words unpacked

  • Pratyaksh — living, present “in blood and flesh,” hearable, question-able, correct-able.
  • Sadguru — True Teacher who bears Verse 10’s five qualities.
  • Paroksh Jina — Omniscient Lord who is not accessible to you (physically absent or geographically inaccessible like Simandharswami in Mahāvideh for us). For us, contact reduces to scripture (śāstra) and idol (mūrti).
  • Upkār — obligation, favour, help actually received by the seeker.

3) Why this verse now? (Era logic and access)

  • We are in the 5th era; no living Tirthankars are present on this earth.
  • Therefore, meeting God (as a living Omniscient) is not possible; meeting Guru is difficult but possible.
  • The highest practical conduit for Dharma in this era is thus a living Sadguru.

4) Scriptures and idols: immense, yet limited for you

  • 110% beneficial in themselves—but two practical limits for a lone reader: 1) You may miss the underlying meaning or distort it (misinterpretation risk). 2) Scripture cannot dispel your personal doubts in real-time or correct blind spots.
  • Result of DIY: delusion → confusion → frustration → depression. The text can’t walk over and say, “You’ve got it wrong, here’s why.”

5) What only a living Sadguru can do

Three I’s: He Imparts (teaches), Inspires (fires love for the Self), and Inspects (points out specific faults and guides correction).

Concrete obligations (upkār) unique to pratyaksh Guru: 1) Diagnoses specifically: detects your pride points, masks, escape routes. 2) Gives ājñā (clear commands) proportioned to your capacity—no options overload. 3) Resolves dilemmas: simplifies, de-conflicts, puts next step under your feet. 4) Corrects in the moment: adjusts posture, method, interpretation while you practise. 5) Binds the heart: through living contact, awakens ātma-prem (love for the Self) which powers sustained practice.

Hence the verse’s comparison: The Gods’ upkār to you today is mediated (text/idol); the Sadguru’s upkār is immediate, tailored, and catalytic.

6) “Greater by upkār, not by state” — removing confusions

  • Not claiming a Sadguru’s inner state equals or exceeds a Tirthankar’s. State comparison is category error here.
  • The verse speaks of which support benefits you more now: accessible living guidance outweighs inaccessible omniscient perfection for the disciple’s progress.

7) The ignition of ātma-vichār (how conviction triggers practice)

Mechanism described in the discourse: 1) Conviction: “A living Sadguru’s upkār is greater for me.” 2) Trust → Surrender: You stop splitting life—“this by my opinions, that by His.” 3) Proximity effect: love and bond make you share, consult, and obey in detail (even “Should I cut my hair?”—an illustration of heartfelt reliance, not mind-weakness). 4) Flame lit: True Self-love (ātma-prem) and Self-contemplation arise; you begin to want not only satsang/sevā but Self-realisation itself.

8) Practical guidance for seekers

A. Form the right conviction (vivek): - Rehearse Verse 11’s lens: “For me, a living Sadguru confers the greater benefit.” - Remember accessibility is part of truth’s delivery system.

B. Align conduct: - Stop mixing: don’t keep a private zone run by opinions while outsourcing “spirituals” to the Guru. - Ask for ājñā; obey without disguised guidance (“May I do it this way?”). - Let love—not fear— move your compliance.

C. Use scripture rightly: - Study what He assigns; bring confusions to Him. Make scripture a bridge, not an echo chamber.

D. When away from the Guru: - Keep contact via assigned texts/practices and regular reporting; translate distance into inner nearness by stricter opinion-dropping.

9) FAQ the talk implicitly answered

  • Q: Is Simandharswami “living” for us? A: No; inaccessible to us ⇒ treated as paroksh (non-present) for our purposes.
  • Q: Are idols and āgams enough? A: They are holy and powerful, but cannot personally dispel your doubts or inspect you.
  • Q: Isn’t this Guru-centric? A: It’s liberation-centric: it prioritises what actually moves you now—living correction, command, and compassion.

10) Illustrations used in the discourse

  • Era framing: 4th era had living Tirthankars; in the 5th, only Guru can preach to us directly.
  • Diary note (Kārtik Pūrṇimā): The Master’s mission is acceptance; correction happens because the disciples desire it and His love complies.
  • “Left-hand job”: Accepting is easy; patient correction proves love—hence inspection is upkār.

11) Errors to avoid

  • Equalising states: mistaking the claim as “Guru = God.” The verse is “greater by upkār,” not ontology.
  • Bypassing the Guru: attempting a scripture-only path while ignoring living guidance.
  • Splitting allegiance: keeping opinion-ruled compartments while seeking fruits of surrender.

12) Micro-practices to live Verse 11

Daily (5 min): 1) Conviction recall: Repeat: “For my progress today, my living Sadguru’s upkār is greatest.”

2) Opinion fast: Pick one small decision you will place under ājñā (instead of DIY).

Weekly (10–15 min): - Doubt dump: List your current confusions; bring them for inspection. Close the loop in writing after the Guru’s response.

Monthly (20 min): - Upkār audit: Journal 3 specific instances this month where living guidance saved time, prevented error, or deepened Self-love.

13) One-page recap

  • Claim: For a seeker today, the living Sadguru’s upkār is greater than the non-present Jina’s.
  • Reason: Accessibility ⇒ immediate, tailored, corrective help (Impart–Inspire–Inspect).
  • Fruit: Only with this conviction does ātma-vichār and true ātma-prem arise.
  • Method: Conviction → Surrender → Proximity → Practice under ājñā → Progress.
  • Guard-rails: Honour scripture and idols deeply, yet take living refuge for clarity and correction.
