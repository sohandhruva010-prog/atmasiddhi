Verse 135 of the Upsamhar. After four verses explaining the balance of Nishchay and Vyavhar, Param Krupalu Dev now introduces another balance that is just as important:

Upadan + Nimit
Principal cause + Instrumental cause

But Gurudev spends the first half of this satsang explaining something deeper before even entering the verse: why we need inspiration at all.

If every soul already possesses the potential of a Siddha, why do we need Guru? Why contemplate Mahavir Bhagwan? Why bhakti? Why external inspiration?

Because having the potential is not the same as manifesting it.

The verse is essentially:

“Sarva jeev chhe Siddh sam, je samje te thay;
Sadguru ajna Jindasha, nimit karan mai.”

Meaning:

All souls are, by their inherent nature/potential, like the Liberated Ones. One who truly realizes this attains that state. The ajna of Sadguru and contemplation of the state of the Jina are powerful instrumental causes toward that liberation. Pasted text(20261116-003625)

Section 12 — Upsamhar
Verse 135: You Have the Potential — But You Need Inspiration to Manifest It

The verse has two beautifully balanced halves:

First line — UPADAN

The potential already exists within YOU.

Second line — NIMIT

Sadguru and the state of the Jina inspire, guide and support the manifestation of that potential.

So neither extreme works:

“Guru will do everything for me.” ❌ nor:

“Everything is already within me, so I need nobody.” ❌

The path requires understanding the proper role of both.

  • comfort,
  • wealth,
  • travel,
  • beautiful lifestyles,
  • opportunities.

Why is there still so much depression and unhappiness?

His answer is:

Our focus shifted from inner grandeur to outer grandeur.

We became fascinated by:

  • wealth,
  • fame,
  • luxury,
  • external achievement,

while losing connection with the greatness within. Pasted text(20261116-003625)

This sets up the entire verse.

You already contain an extraordinary spiritual potential.

2. What does “inspiration” actually mean?

Getting elevated.

Becoming motivated to explore new possibilities.

Receiving a burst of new energy.

Inspiration breaks us out of:

  • fixed thinking,
  • intellectual prejudices,
  • emotional hang-ups,
  • repetitive mental loops.

Without inspiration, we remain inside our own little cocoon:

“This is how I think.”

“This is how life is.”

“This is what makes me happy.”

“This is what I want.”

Inspiration creates a paradigm shift:

Maybe I can become something far greater than what I currently believe myself to be. Pasted text(20261116-003625)

18. Every EFFECT requires a corresponding CAUSE

Gurudev introduces the basic law of causation:

Every effect has a corresponding cause.

If you churn water for hours:

no butter.

Why?

Not because you didn't work hard.

Because water isn't the corresponding cause for butter.

For any effect to arise, appropriate causes must exist. Pasted text(20261116-003625)

And Gurudev says two broad categories of cause are widely recognized:

1. Upadan Karan

Principal/material cause.

2. Nimit Karan

Instrumental/associating cause.

19. What is UPADAN?

Gurudev defines it:

The substance that itself undergoes modification to become the effect.

Example:

Milk → curd

What actually transforms into curd?

Milk.

Therefore:

Milk = Upadan / material cause. Pasted text(20261116-003625)

20. The POT example

Suppose the effect is:

a pot.

What actually becomes the pot?

Clay.

Therefore:

Clay = Upadan.

What helps clay transform?

  • potter,
  • wheel,
  • stick,
  • associated conditions.

These are:

Nimit.

Once the pot is formed:

  • potter leaves,
  • wheel leaves,
  • stick leaves.

But clay remains as the substance of the pot.

So:

Upadan = what transforms.

Nimit = what assists the transformation. Pasted text(20261116-003625)
