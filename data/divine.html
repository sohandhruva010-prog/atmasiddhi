2025

USA Dharmayatra

Three weeks with my Guru. Absolutely unbelievable.

Weekend 1. HE looked straight at me and smiled when PKD came pratyaksh. BAPA was walking behind PKD’s car and saw me dancing with my whole heart, to my fullest. In that moment I felt so happy and free of every extra thought, and that is exactly when HE looked at me.

After the event, as HE was getting into the buggy, HE looked at me and gave me that smirky smile, as if asking whether I really liked it and enjoyed the event.

Weekend 2. I had the blessed opportunity to have lunch with HIM for graduation. This was a divine interaction.

First, Nemiji made me eat spicy food. It was hilarious, because my reaction made the whole room laugh, including BAPA. Nemiji gave me a bruschetta covered in Da Bomb sauce, and my overconfident self said, “Haan, I’ll eat anything you want,” as if I could handle it. BAPA saw all of it.

Nemiji asked my name and where I was from, and it felt like BAPA nodded as if HE already knew and was recollecting. Nemiji also asked, “Oh, tu Mehulbhai no chokro che? Your mom is in PMT.”

After BAPA signed the certificates, HE had everyone take a picture with HIM. This was divine. HE looked at my certificate, then at me, and said, “Accha, toh tu su bhane che?” I said, “Actually BAPA, it is chemical engineering. Khabar nahi aayu, material sciences su kaam lakhyu che.” Very divine.

Then HE asked, “Toh tu ketla varas no thayo?” I answered, “BAPA, 22.” HE said, “Oh, tu toh bahu nano che, aa chokriyo bahu moti che tari maate.” Then HE took a picture and blessed me.

This is all I can remember. I am sure there was more.

SF Dharmayatra

At the airport. We first met HIM at the airport. HE looked only at me and talked to me. HE asked, “Tu Phoenix?” (looking at Nemiji for confirmation) “Tu aaya su kare che?” I said, “Tamne madva aavyo chu.” HE smiled and nodded.

The retreat. We did Atmasiddhi garba during the retreat, and I had the blessing of playing dandiya with HIM two or three times. During verse 38, HE looked at me and smiled:

Kashaay ni upashaantataa, maatra moksh abhilaash.
Bhave khed praani daya, tyaa atmaarth nivaas.

॥38॥

Then I had the privilege of sitting a few feet away from HIM and simply observing HIM. Again HE looked at me and smiled.

Mylapore. Then the unexpected. My brother, Prateek, and I randomly decided to go to Mylapore. Really, it was HIM calling us there. We saw HIM and were truly shocked. After finishing HIS meal, HE came straight to us without any hesitation and said, “This seems like a hot spot for all the Jains. They took me here to eat some food, although in India we get Jain sambar everywhere.”

I asked, “What did you eat, BAPA?” HE lovingly said, “I had idli dipped in sambar, and a little bit of dosa.” I asked, “Idli soft hati ke?” HE didn’t quite say yes; HE said, “Haan.” Then HE smiled lovingly and walked away. The amazing part: HE patiently waited for the other folks to come in. It was truly special.

Saying goodbye. At the airport, HE came straight to us, looked at me, and said, “I got your dad’s email.” I was so confused and had no idea what HE meant. HE said, “Mehul ne.” I nodded. HE said, “Yeah, he was very happy that I met you guys at the restaurant.”

In that moment my mind went blank, and all I said was, “BAPA…” HE looked at me, eagerly listening. “Can I get a hug?” And HE lovingly wrapped HIS arms around me.

Then HE looked at the entire SF group and said, “Je jene himmat thay, ene made.”

Those couple of days were truly insane.

Los Angeles

June

I again had the privilege of talking to HIM. I had a broken hand. HE looked at it with wide eyes and said, “What happened?!” In broken English I said, “BAPA, I broke my hand,” as if HE couldn’t see it.

Then I handed HIM two plaques and asked if HE would sign them. HE signed one “Love you,” pointing to my name.

I also asked, “BAPA, can you sign my hand please?” HE laughed and smiled and said, “I will sign in the ashram.” Then HE asked me, “You are coming, no!?” I said yes. Such a simple but powerful interaction.

Ashram

July

This was insane. I could only go on weekends. The first weekend was busy with café seva; the second weekend was the GPS celebrations.

On the last day, I had the privilege of going up to touch HIS feet on stage. In that moment HE is extremely still; HE doesn’t react or talk to anyone. But HE looked at me and gave me an ishara: first HE pointed at me, then signaled next, and then made an “L” sign. Next PYG Lead. I was shocked, and honestly a little confused.

Later, as HE was walking around blessing everyone, HE looked at me, smiled, and said, “Behave, or I will break your other hand as well.”

Two or three weeks later, since I did not behave, I dislocated my shoulder for the third time.

Shikharji Yatra

September

Mumbai Airport. This was insane, again. We had a strong feeling BAPA would be on the same flight as us. HE came in, and like a celebrity, everyone crowded around HIM. I had zero confidence HE would even look at me: first, because there were so many people, and second, because since July I had only been doing nonsense and had drained the life out of myself.

So I moved in front of HIM and was casually talking with my dad and Chintan Chheda. BAPA was sitting directly in front of us, right in my line of sight. HE looked at me with HIS hand on HIS chin and a surprised expression, as if to say, oh, it’s you. Someone stepped in front of HIM and blocked the view, and even then HE moved HIS head slightly to the right to keep looking at me. Because I was mid-conversation, I only caught a glimpse of this when I glanced over.

Then my dad said, “Go say thank you to HIM.” So my nervous self went to say thank you. Before I could say anything, HE looked at me, held out HIS hand for a handshake, and said “Hello” in a stylish tone. I said, “Hi BAPA,” touched HIS feet, and sat down on the floor.

I was truly speechless, just smiling. HE looked at me and said, “Pune to Phoenix” — not a question, more a statement. I nodded.

Then HE said, “You were my choice,” and smiled, while I just kept smiling back.

Then, with an intense look, HE said, “I am always assessing you,” and then smiled lovingly and gave my left cheek a smack. Truly unexpected and divine. HE answered every question I had and instilled such confidence in me. It was amazing.

On the plane. My mom lovingly asked HIM, “Shata ma cho?” and HE just talked with the three of us, Mom, Dad, and me, so lovingly and casually. It was truly special; I cannot put it into words. Later, as we were walking, HE said to Nemiji, “Phoenix.”

Shikharji day: the trail. After a grueling but amazing hike, my brother and I were truly in the moment, fully present, and some divine things happened.

At a crossroads where everyone else was following the main trail, a man at a stall confidently told us to take the other trail, saying it would lead to the Parasnath temple. We said, screw it, let’s go. If we get scammed, so be it, it will be fun.

We went, and out of nowhere we saw BAPA. So many things had to go right. The hike was 27 km long; even two minutes late, or a delay anywhere along the way, and we would have missed HIM. But at that exact moment, we were at the junction leading to the Parasnath temple.

We saw BAPA and simply waited for HIS darshan, expecting nothing; just seeing HIM was more than enough. HE looked at me and said nothing, but made a gesture like oof, this is a lot and I’m tired, waving HIS hand and shaking HIS head. I gestured back, come on BAPA, you got this!, with a fist up. HE smiled so lovingly, even tired and a little exhausted. Then HE gave us the ishara, come on guys, let’s go, and we followed HIM.

In the temple. Amid the chaos and the crazy crowd, BAPA was in dhyan with HIS eyes closed, in full bhakti mode. I was blessed just to see HIM. Right as I was leaving the temple room, HE opened HIS eyes, took a handful of rice, and threw it directly at me. It was insane, and it felt like it was just for me.
